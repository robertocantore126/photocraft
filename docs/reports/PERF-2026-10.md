# Performance work, October 2026

Base: `8c0d36b` (scratch disk, damage diff, UI fixes). This tree integrates three lanes: the
compositor (B), canvas scheduling (C) and a lazy native format (A). Every number below was
measured on `finallynewopera.psb` (3992×4990, 479 layers, 54 with layer effects; one
document-wide group, "Group 1", has its own effects) on an RTX 3060 / Vulkan, 15.8 GB RAM,
scratch disk on a USB SSD.

## Results

| Operation | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Brush drag, top-level layer (frame) | 43 ms | 13–14 ms (≈ 72 fps) |
| Brush drag, layer inside "Group 1" (frame) | 58 ms (1,000–1,600 ms before lane B) | 17–21 ms |
| Full refresh (warm) | 233 ms | 138 ms |
| Zoomed-out refresh (mip 2) | — | 46–48 ms |
| Open the native file | — (PSB import 19–22 s) | 0.2–0.4 s |
| Whole image on screen after opening | ≈ 30 s (import + first frame) | ≈ 1.6 s (preview, no effects) |
| Image complete after opening | — | ≈ 5 s, one 2.5 s stall |
| Save as native (first time) | > 12 min, never finished | 10.0 s, 2.69 GB |
| Save, no changes | — | 37 ms |
| Save after a one-pixel edit | — | 39 ms (1 tile written) |
| Native round trip identical to the PSB import | no (70 layers differed) | yes |

Frame times come from the app's own frame log during a 60-move brush drag through the
control channel (`ui.pointer`). Do not time the app by polling it: a `ui.inspect` costs it
0.6–5.6 s on this document, and a `ui.screenshot` ~550 ms. Headless numbers come from
`region_performance_probe`.

## What changed

### Compositor (crate `gpu`)

- **Pass culling** (lane B): a plain raster Content/Blend pair whose tiles miss a chunk is
  skipped there, through logical-to-physical slot aliases. Its pages are neither checked nor
  uploaded for that cell.
- **Chunks are whole page cells** (2048, natively; 1024 on the web). Every chunk runs every pass
  of the plan, and a pass costs about the same over a quarter or a whole cell: a 1100×500
  region crossing the 1024 grid paid 6 chunks. 884 → 325 passes, 41 → 31 ms.
- **Effect maps outside the refresh are left alone**: a stroke re-checked all 54 effect
  layers' maps (4 ms). Maps are only read inside their region, so a layer whose effects, new and
  cached, miss the refresh keeps them until a refresh reaches them.
- **A group's effect maps update incrementally.** Its key no longer hashes every descendant
  tile (4 ms per refresh). Each descendant surface keeps a tile snapshot, and a change re-renders
  the shape over the changed pixels only, grown by that descendant's effect reach (its own and
  its parent groups') and its mask feather. Changed tiles are diffed to the pixel.
- **A group's shape is composited on the GPU**: a nested render of the group alone, without
  its effects, at full opacity over transparency, read back. It reuses the resident pages. The
  CPU composited every descendant per pixel: 0.86 s for 0.55 MP of "Group 1". Artboards and
  pixels outside the canvas stay on the CPU. The refresh submits what it recorded before the
  nested render, which reads the children's effect maps.
- **Effect maps are full float** (were half). The GPU group shape matched the CPU's within 4e-4,
  yet 8 pixels of a glow tail fell on the other side of the 0.5/255 inside threshold, and each
  grew a stroke speck (130/255). With R32F maps: no flips.
- **Plan prefix cache.** While a brush stroke changes one layer, the passes before that layer's
  first pass are composited once over the page cell, and the slots still read after it are
  kept. Each later refresh starts there: 484 of 648 passes skipped for a top-level layer of the
  PSB. Validity is checked per refresh: each pass's settings signature, plus the versions of
  the pages and effect maps it samples. A snapshot is built after two refreshes in a row change
  the same pass. Building it binds every page of the cell, not only the ones the chunks need.
  `PHOTOCRAFT_PREFIX_CACHE=0` turns it off.
- **The plan is built once per refresh**, not twice (the canvas checked `supports` first).
  Effect gradients are prepared once per table instead of per sample: planning the PSB went
  12.9 → 2.9 ms.
- **Live strokes vouch for pixels only** (`set_pixels_only`, from a live stroke's second frame):
  groups keep their settings key instead of formatting 400 layers' settings per dab (3.5 ms).
  Small group shapes (area × descendant surfaces ≤ 2 M) stay on the CPU, where they cost less
  than a GPU round trip.
- **The effect-map cache gets two fifths of the GPU budget** (at most 2 GB), and the shape's
  CPU copy no longer counts against it. With a quarter (1 GB) the PSB's 1.3 GB of maps were
  rebuilt on every slice.
- **Adjustment LUT textures are cached** by content hash (they were rebuilt and uploaded every
  refresh: frame resources 2 → 0.3 ms).
- **Mip compositing** (lane B): `render_at_mip` composites at levels 1–8 from GPU layer mip
  chains. Reduced-resolution blending is approximate; level 0 is exact.

### Canvas (crate `ui-egui`, lane C)

- Large refreshes (opening, full re-renders, damage over 4 MP) go in slices of one page cell,
  the view centre first, one per frame. Strokes and other small damage are composited at once.
- Lane C's 256-pixel pages and adaptive 64–512 px slices were dropped: slices smaller than a
  cell only multiply the passes, and switching page sizes dropped every cached texture of every
  open document.
- Per-cell effect maps while slicing (`Compositor::set_progressive`) stay available but unused:
  a document-wide group cost 4.3 s per stroke that way, against 1.7 s for whole-region maps
  (before the GPU group shape).
- A coarse preview at the view's mip level shows the whole image first when zoomed out below
  50%. It leaves layer effects out: building their maps was most of the first slice (3.9 s on
  the PSB). The exact slices draw them (`PHOTOCRAFT_PREVIEW_EFFECTS=1` keeps them).

### Native format (crate `format`, lane A)

- `.pcraft` is an append-only pack: LZ4 tiles with CRC32, a JSON manifest and a CRC'd footer per
  commit, synced before the footer is written. Opening reads the manifest only; tiles load on
  demand from the file. A save appends only the tiles that are new since the last commit.
- **Automatic compaction**: when a save leaves more superseded bytes than live ones, and at
  least 64 MB of them, the pack is rewritten compactly (to a temporary file, then renamed).
- **Exact numbers in the manifest**: serde_json's `float_roundtrip`. Without it every save/open
  moved shape paths, transforms and effect reference points by one ULP (also in the old format).
- **Slices keep their group name** when their list is empty (the old format dropped it).
- **Closing a document releases its file.** A process-wide blob cache held its pack (and so
  the file handle) strongly until the next save or load. It now holds it weakly.
- PSD/PSB stay import/export formats.

## Verification

- `photocraft-gpu`: 12 unit tests; parity 30/31. The failure is the existing Hue/Saturation 1/255
  difference (`adjustment_layers`), also on `main`. New: `group_effects_update_incrementally`
  (dabs beside a glowing child, on a feathered mask, in a nested group with effects; erase; a
  setting change), each compared with the CPU and asserting a partial rebuild.
- `photocraft-ui-egui`: 355 unit tests plus `adjust_preview_gpu`, `canvas_16f`,
  `color_managed_canvas` and `progressive_canvas`. `color_managed_canvas` crashes in the driver
  about one run in two (0xc0000005), as on `main`; it passes when it runs.
- `photocraft-format`, `-raster`, `-io`: all pass, with a unit test for the compaction threshold.
- PSB round trip: `pack_probe` imports the PSB, saves, reopens and compares the documents:
  equal.

## Open

- The first exact slice after opening builds every effect map in one go (CPU distance fields):
  a 2.5 s stall. A GPU distance transform (jump flooding) would remove it, and the remaining
  ~5 ms of a stroke inside a group with effects.
- Layer thumbnails read most of each visible layer to sample 64×64 pixels (~2 s on opening the
  PSB). They could be built in the background.
- Memory Usage matters: at 4096 MB (half of it for tiles) this 6.6 GB-decoded document reloaded
  tiles 37,000 times while opening. At 8192 MB, 12,000.
- Native saves write no thumbnail or composite preview (nothing in the app reads them yet).
